Recommended Actions

  • Review older promotional references and confirm whether they are still relevant.
  • Check potentially expired offers before keeping them on the page.
  • Review seasonal wording such as limited-time or summer-sale claims.
  • Fix broken internal links and replace unnecessary redirects where appropriate.
  • Verify that visible offers match the page's structured data and metadata.
